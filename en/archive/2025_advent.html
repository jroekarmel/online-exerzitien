Mariam, or the Way of the Lord's Poor

Advent 2025 with St. Mariam of Jesus Crucified

from November 26 to December 25 2025

Saint Mariam of Jesus Crucified (1846-1878), the "Little Arab," born in the Holy Land, a humble Carmelite who learned to read and write as an adult, founded Carmelite monasteries in the Holy Land and India. Her simple and radiant life, led by the Holy Spirit, invites us to let ourselves be loved and transformed by Jesus.
