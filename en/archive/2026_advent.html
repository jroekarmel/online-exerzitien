The Practice of the Presence of God

Advent 2026 with Brother Lawrence of the Resurrection

from November 25 to January 3 2027

Brother Laurentius of the Resurrection, a understated figure of the Carmelite Order, was one of the great masters of the spiritual life in the 17th century. His teachings, recently brought back into the spotlight by Pope Leo XIV, remain remarkably relevant today. This humble lay brother teaches us to place the presence of God at the center of our daily lives. His doctrine is rooted in the great Christian tradition and responds to Jesus’ call to “pray without ceasing” (Luke 18:1), in accordance with Saint Paul’s exhortation: “Pray without interruption” (1 Thessalonians 5:17). For Brother Lawrence, the way is simple: to remain at every moment in the presence of God, whatever our occupations may be. In this Advent season, we invite you to discover this school of the presence of God, so as to grow in a relationship that is simpler, more confident, and more intimate with the Lord.
