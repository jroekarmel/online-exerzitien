Encountering Christ in the Depths of Our Hearts

Carmelite Online Lenten Retreat 2026 with Saint John of the Cross

from February 18 to April 5 2026

We invite you to take part in an online retreat guided by Saint John of the Cross. Throughout this retreat, we will deepen our relationship with Jesus in the light of the Gospels. In the Holy Spirit, we will rediscover our intimacy with him and with the Father, in the very depths of our hearts. This retreat will be a path of interior liberation, with John of the Cross as our sure guide. In a world that is increasingly turned outward, he will teach us to rediscover our deepest heart, where Christ, Bridegroom of the Church, desires to meet us and to purify us. Through this purification, in the fire of the Holy Spirit, we may become the beloved children of the Father with him.
