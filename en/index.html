Current Retreat

Advent 2026 with Brother Laurence of the Resurrection

Brother Laurentius of the Resurrection, a understated figure of the Carmelite Order, was one of the great masters of the spiritual life in the 17th century. His teachings, recently brought back into the spotlight by Pope Leo XIV, remain remarkably relevant today.

This humble lay brother teaches us to place the presence of God at the center of our daily lives. His doctrine is rooted in the great Christian tradition and responds to Jesus’ call to “pray without ceasing” (Luke 18:1), in accordance with Saint Paul’s exhortation: “Pray without interruption” (1 Thessalonians 5:17). For Brother Lawrence, the way is simple: to remain at every moment in the presence of God, whatever our occupations may be.

In this Advent season, we invite you to discover this school of the presence of God, so as to grow in a relationship that is simpler, more confident, and more intimate with the Lord.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is an online retreat?

The online retreat is designed to help deepen our spiritual relationship with Jesus in light of the Gospels. For this purpose, reflections will be sent by email during each week of Advent and Lent.

How often will I receive messages?

You will receive a welcome email and then weekly emails during advent and lent.

Does participation cost anything?

Regristation is free. Donations are welcome to support our work.

I didn't receive an email - what schould I do?

Please check your spam folder. If you missed any materials, you can also browse the archive on this website.
